YOUR GRANT-WRITING INSTRUCTOR

Meet Nichole Abraham

Nichole Abraham is the instructor and founder of Abraham Grant Academy.

Her goal is to make the grant-writing process understandable, approachable and practical for beginners, entrepreneurs, community organizations and aspiring grant writers.

Through practical education, free lessons and upcoming seminars, Abraham Grant Academy is designed to help students develop the knowledge and confidence needed to pursue funding opportunities.
